MOUTH

Drag anywhere in the mouth panel to move the vowel: front ↔ back, close ↕ open; the dotted box marks the corner vowels i u æ ɑ, outside it goes past them. The cross is where you set it, the dot where Motion takes it. TEXT sings words: a syllable per note (GATE), a word per note (ONE-SHOT) or a syllable per beat (LOOP); it applies as you type, Enter or Escape gives the keys back.

CONTROLS

Knobs and value boxes: drag up or down; double-click resets; click a knob's value to type it. ♪ locks a time to the tempo. Amber values are past the human anatomy, which is allowed.

SEQUENCER

A step: click turns it on or off, drag up or down for semitones, shift-click ties it to the next, double-click returns to the root. The bar under the note is its length (drag), ◆ accents it, and the lower half picks its vowel (click). Keys transpose the pattern. With a file, the sequencer gates the sound; FOLDS sings the notes' pitch.
